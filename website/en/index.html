Made for editing contacts in bulk

Whether you have dozens of contacts or hundreds, Bearoster helps you edit fields, apply tags, change groups, and roll back mistakes.

Edit contacts in bulk

Update names, companies, departments, job titles, phone numbers, email addresses, postal addresses, and birthdays for multiple contacts at once. Review who will be affected, then fill blank fields or replace existing values.

4

Organize with tags

Use local tags to group clients, family, colleagues, or project contacts. Nest tags into a hierarchy, filter your list, or use them to choose contacts for a bulk edit.

Drag contacts between groups

Drag a contact from the list to an Apple Contacts group to change its group. Select several contacts first to move the whole group at once.

Cache and edit history

Edits are saved to a local cache first, along with a history of your actions. If something’s wrong, undo it or roll back. Save to Contacts only when the result looks right.

Review before you save

Bearoster keeps a local record between your edits and Contacts. Preview each change, check the result, and undo anything you don’t want before writing to the system address book.

Birthdays in multiple calendars

Manage birthdays in Gregorian, Lunar, Hebrew, and Islamic calendars. Upcoming dates appear in the calendar and list, with a color for each calendar.

Your data stays on your Mac

Bearoster doesn’t upload your contacts or collect usage data. The first time you open it, macOS asks you to grant Contacts access.

  • Every read and write uses the system Contacts framework
  • Your cache and edit history stay in your user directory
  • No in-app purchases, subscriptions, or ads
Contacts access only No uploads, no account, no cloud
You can revoke access at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Contacts.

Frequently asked questions

Will Bearoster change my contacts right away?

No. Your edits go into a local cache and history first. Bearoster writes them to Contacts only after you confirm, and you can undo or roll back changes before then.

How are Lunar, Hebrew, and Islamic birthdays calculated?

Bearoster uses Foundation’s Calendar API to convert non-Gregorian dates to their Gregorian date for the current year. It uses that date for sorting, reminders, and the calendar view.

Will my changes sync through iCloud?

Bearoster doesn’t manage syncing. If iCloud is enabled for Contacts, iCloud handles changes to your system contacts as usual. Bearoster handles the editing on your Mac.

Why does Bearoster need Contacts access?

The app needs permission to edit contacts in bulk, read groups, and save your changes. Without it, Bearoster can show only an empty state or content from its local cache.

Will Bearoster charge later?

The current version is free, with no subscription or in-app purchases. Local features will not be locked.
