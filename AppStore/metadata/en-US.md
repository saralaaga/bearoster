## Description (4000 max)

```
Bearoster is a macOS contacts tool that runs entirely on your Mac, with birthdays as its focus.

It reads the real data in your system Contacts and writes back to that same data. Nothing is imported, copied, or uploaded — edits show up in the Contacts app immediately.

BIRTHDAYS
· Four calendars: Gregorian, Chinese lunar, Hebrew, and Islamic
· Month view so you can see at a glance who has a birthday coming up
· Birthday list sorted by next birthday, with filters for upcoming dates
· Voice input: say "March fifteenth" and it becomes the right date

ORGANIZING CONTACTS
· A tag system with nested subtags, like "Friends / College"
· Filter by Apple groups, tags, and missing fields — stack multiple conditions and match all or any of them
· Edit many contacts at once, with a preview of every change before you save
· Fills in blanks only. It will not overwrite what you already wrote unless you ask it to

WHY IT'S SAFE
· Your contacts never leave your Mac — no uploads, no syncing, no third-party servers
· No data collection. No analytics, no telemetry, no third-party SDKs
· No account, no sign-in
· Just two permissions: Contacts (read and write), and Microphone + Speech Recognition (only used when you start voice input)

REQUIREMENTS
macOS 14 or later.
```
